# Java grid interconnection in IndoTERM-E

Discussion paper for the team (draft v1, 13 October 2026): how the idea of connecting the Java-Bali grid to the outer islands could be simulated with IndoTERM-E, in a static and a dynamic set-up.

Open `index.html` in a browser. The page is self-contained: figures are embedded, equations are rendered with MathML, and it has a contents sidebar, links from the `[C..]` tags to the numbers register in Appendix C, and a light/dark switch.

Source: `IndoTERM-E_Java_grid_interconnection_team_discussion_v1.docx` (converted with pandoc).

## Additions on 16 October 2026 (not in the docx)

- **Section 5.6, Costs and wider benefits of a link:** capital cost per MW of new plants against grid links, cost per MWh delivered for a Bali–Lombok case, and benefits beyond money saved. Screening estimates; sources and assumptions are in the figure captions.
- **Appendix D2, Literature support from OpenAlex:** 60 entries in two groups. Group 1 covers the relevance of, and rationale for, grid connection (1a–1e). Group 2 covers CGE and other economy-wide models with the same aim. Each entry has its DOI, OpenAlex link, citation count and the claim it supports.
- **Appendix D:** OpenAlex links and citation counts added to every reference with a DOI.

Nothing has been simulated yet. This is a basis for discussion, not a result.
